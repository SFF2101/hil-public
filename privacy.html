HIL Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

HIL Drive is a private, non-commercial application for its account owner's household information backups. This public website contains no household records.

Access and use

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file OAuth permission only. It uses that access to establish a dedicated archive folder, create encrypted backup objects, inspect their metadata, retrieve them to verify integrity, and test isolated recovery. It does not request unrestricted Drive access. It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or AI model training.

Storage and security

Backup record contents are encrypted before upload to the owner's Google Drive account. Authentication credentials and encryption keys are retained privately, not on this website or in public source code. Initial backup and recovery validation uses synthetic records. The operator controls subsequent use and access.

Sharing and retention

The application does not sell or intentionally disclose Google user data to unrelated third parties. Google provides the storage and authentication services subject to the account owner's Google terms and settings. Archive copies remain until the account owner takes a retention or deletion action. The application does not automatically delete archive objects or local originals.

Control and contact

The account owner may revoke access through Google Account security settings and delete application-created files in Drive. Contact the operator using the support email displayed on the OAuth consent screen.

Terms of use

This application is for authorized personal use only. It is not offered as a commercial service. The account owner controls access and recovery and remains responsible for the Google account and its security settings.
