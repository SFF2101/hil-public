HIL Drive

HIL Drive is a private, non-commercial backup application for personal household information management. It is not a public service.

Its purpose is to copy encrypted local records to a dedicated application-managed Google Drive archive, independently check the copied files, and verify recovery without replacing the local originals. Initial testing uses synthetic records.

Google Drive access

The application requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission for files it creates or that the account owner specifically authorizes. It does not request general access to unrelated Drive files.
